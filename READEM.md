🍫💚 Healthy Green Chocolate with Pumpkin Seeds

:women_cook: Introduction

This project focuses on producing a healthy, naturally green chocolate using pumpkin seeds and green tea powder (Matcha).
It contains no artificial colors or additives, keeping the product natural and 
nutritious.

 ⚙️Preparation Steps

1. Grind pumpkin seeds into fine powder.

2. Melt cocoa butter over low heat.

3. Add matcha powder and honey, stir until smooth.

4. Add ground pumpkin seeds and vanilla extract, continue mixing.

5. Pour the mixture into molds.

6. Refrigerate until solid

🌿 Result: Smooth, naturally green chocolate with a rich, healthy flavor.
