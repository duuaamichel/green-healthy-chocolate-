


🍀 Types of Healthy Green Chocolate:

1. Dark Green Chocolate (high cocoa content) 
   - Rich in antioxidants, strong chocolate flavor.


2. Green Milk Chocolate
   - Smooth, creamy, lighter taste.


3. Green Chocolate with Hazelnuts
   - Crunchy texture, nutty flavor.


4. Green Chocolate with Mint Flavor
   - Refreshing, pleasant aftertaste.


5. Green Chocolate with Dried Fruits
   - Natural sweetness, chewy texture.
