

🧾 Ingredients:
 Composition of Green Chocolate with Pumpkin Seeds

🧾 Main Ingredients:

100g cocoa butter – base for smooth texture

50g cocoa powder – rich chocolate flavor

30g ground pumpkin seeds – source of protein and minerals

2 tablespoons natural honey – natural sweetener

1 teaspoon matcha or spirulina powder – gives the natural green color

1 teaspoon natural vanilla extract – aroma enhancer

A pinch of sea salt – balances sweetness
---

🌿 Optional Ingredients:

10g grated coconut – adds crunch and aroma

Chopped nuts (almonds, hazelnuts) – for texture and healthy fats

Dried fruits (cranberries, dates) – natural sweetness boost

---

🧠 Why These Ingredients:

Pumpkin seeds add zinc, magnesium, and protein.

Matcha powder provides antioxidants and natural green color.

Cocoa butter keeps the chocolate smooth and creamy.

Honey or date syrup offers sweetness without refined sugar.

Vanilla and sea salt balance flavor and aroma.

---

💚 Result:
A creamy, naturally green chocolate bar full of nutrients, flavor, and wellness 🌱🍫.
